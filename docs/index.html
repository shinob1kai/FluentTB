Features What FluentTB brings to your taskbar

⬛

Rounded Corners

Add a smooth corner radius to your taskbar — from subtle to fully rounded.

↔️

Custom Margins

Set individual top, bottom, left and right margins. Use negative values to pin the taskbar to a screen edge.

Auto Updates

FluentTB checks for updates every 24 hours in the background and notifies you via Windows notification. One click installs.

🖥️

Tray Control

Show or hide the system clock and tray at any time. Quick toggle with Win+F2. Also supports hover-to-reveal.

🪟

Fill on Maximize

Automatically expands the taskbar back to full width when a window is maximised or the Task Switcher opens.

🎨

TranslucentTB Compatible

Works side-by-side with TranslucentTB 2021.5+ for blur and transparency effects on your floating taskbar.

How to use Up and running in under a minute

1

Install & Launch

Run the installer and launch FluentTB. It appears in your system tray — right-click the icon to open settings.

2

Adjust Margin & Corner Radius

Use the Margin slider to shrink the taskbar on all sides, and the Corner radius slider to round the corners.

3

Fine-tune with Advanced Options

Click Advanced to set each side's margin independently, toggle tray visibility, enable TranslucentTB compatibility, or control fill-on-maximize behavior.

4

Apply & Done

Hit Apply to save. Settings persist across reboots. Use Win+F2 to quickly toggle the system tray at any time.

5

Updates happen automatically

FluentTB checks for updates every 24 hours in the background. When one is available you'll get a Windows notification — just click it to install.
